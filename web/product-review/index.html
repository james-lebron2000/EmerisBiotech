What this preview can support

A discussion about paired-assay review and reproducible data handoff. It cannot support a clinical treatment decision or demonstrate clinical benefit.

Inputs
Authored synthetic viable-cell measurements, exact patient/sample/molecule mapping and data availability cutoffs. Clinical and omics context is displayed, not fitted.
Outputs
Normalized assay viability, measured/interpolated/extrapolated phases, descriptive cohort distributions and source-preserving exports.
Verification
JSON templates carry checksums. An offline command reproduces supported fixtures and rejects inconsistent exports. Checksums do not establish identity, scientific approval or signatures.
Data residency
Saved single-patient records stay in this browser. Cohort exports are downloaded locally. Clearing browser storage deletes saved browser records. No patient upload or shared team account is provided.
